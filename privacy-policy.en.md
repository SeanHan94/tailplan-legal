# TailPlan Privacy Policy

Effective: October 31, 2026

TailPlan (the "App") helps families care for their pets together. This policy explains what we collect and why, who can see it, and how it is stored and deleted.

## 1. Information we collect

- Account: email address, password (stored hashed; the operator cannot see it), account identifier
- Profile: nickname, chosen color
- Home: home name, invite code, members and roles
- Pets: name, species, breed, birthday, adoption date, sex, neutered status, photo, registration number, allergies and conditions, regular vet, insurance details
- Care records: routine checks, notes, stool, symptoms, temperature, weight, medicine and supplements, vaccines and deworming, vet visits (symptoms, diagnosis, prescription, cost, photos)
- Schedules, expenses and supplies: schedules and places, expense amounts and details, items, purchases
- **Location: route, distance and time while a walk is in progress.** Your location is shown on the map while you view the Walk tab, but a route is saved only after you start a walk.
- Photos: only photos you choose or take are uploaded. The App does not access your whole photo library.
- Notifications: device push token, notification settings, received notifications, app language
- Stored only on your device (not sent to the operator): display mode, today's routines for the widget

The App does not collect advertising identifiers or contacts.

## 2. How we use it

- Sign-up, sign-in, email verification, password reset
- Sharing care records, schedules and expenses within a home, and preventing duplicate care
- Recording walk routes and sharing them live with your family
- Sending routine, schedule, vaccine, restock, family activity and emergency SOS notifications
- Calculating breed rankings (if you join)
- Restoring data on a new device or reinstall, and showing home screen widgets

## 3. What other people can see

- **Members of your home** can see the home's pet details, care records, schedules, expenses, supplies and walk records. **During a walk, your live route and location are visible to them.**
- **Breed rankings (off by default):** if an owner or manager turns on rankings for a pet, other App users can see the **pet's name, breed, distance and number of walks**. Routes, locations, photos and personal details are never shown. Turning it off removes the pet from the public list right away.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- We keep your information until you delete your account, which you can do anytime in Settings → Account → Delete Account.
- Deleting your account immediately deletes your login, user document and received notifications.
- **If other members remain in the home, the records you created stay as shared care history and your name appears as "Former member".** When the last member deletes their account, the home and all its data and photos are deleted.
- Received notifications are deleted automatically after 30 days. Push tokens are discarded when you sign out or delete your account.
- The operator keeps no separate backup copies.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell personal information. Apart from section 3, we do not provide it to third parties except where required by law.

## 6. Service providers and international transfer

- Providers: Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage for Firebase, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging), Apple Inc. (Apple Push Notification service, Apple Maps)
- Purpose: authentication, data storage and sync, photo storage, notifications, maps and place search
- Locations: database and server functions in South Korea (Seoul), **photos in the United States (us-east1)**, and other countries where Google and Apple operate data centers
- Data: the information in section 1, except what is stored only on your device
- When and how: sent over the network when you use the service
- Retention: as described in section 4

Place searches and the location needed for maps are sent to Apple.

## 7. Your rights

- You can view, edit and delete your profile and records in the App.
- You can turn off location and notification access in iPhone Settings anytime. Walk tracking is unavailable without location access.
- You can request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing by deleting your account or contacting us below.

## 8. Security

- All communication is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
- Passwords are hashed by Firebase Authentication.
- Security rules limit a home's data to its members.

## 9. Children

The App is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
